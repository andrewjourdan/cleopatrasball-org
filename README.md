# The Four Hebs · Cleopatra's Ball

**Website:** [www.cleopatrasball.org](https://www.cleopatrasball.org)
**Inquiries:** inquiry@cleopatrasball.org

The Four Hebs is a concept for a seasonal series of performances of William Shakespeare's *Antony and Cleopatra*, staged at each change of season across four locations:

| Heb | Season | City | Stage and gala |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Spring | Manhattan | Delacorte Theater; light show at Cleopatra's Needle; ball at the Temple of Dendur |
| II | Summer | London | Cleopatra's Needle, Victoria Embankment |
| III | Autumn | Paris | Obélisque de Louxor; Théâtre de Verdure du Jardin Shakespeare; ball beneath the Louvre Pyramid |
| IV | Winter | Luxor and Giza | Luxor Museum and Luxor Temple; Nile voyage north; Giza pyramid complex stage |

Each season is planned to raise funds for the theaters, parks, museums and preservation organizations of its host city.
